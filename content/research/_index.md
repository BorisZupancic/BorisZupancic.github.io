Here you will find a short description of my research interests and experience, as well as a list of my completed theses/dissertations, publications, preprints, posters, talks, and links to attached documents.

### Abstract

My current research focus is on my PhD thesis topic: *Resolution of Singularities in Poisson Geometry (and Mathematical Physics).* My supervisor is [Brent Pym](https://www.math.mcgill.ca/bpym/).

Prior to my PhD, I completed an MSc (Project) in mathematics at Queen's University in Kingston, ON, under the supervision of [Thomas Barthelmé](https://www.queensu.ca/mathstat/people/faculty/profiles/barthelme). My dissertation was *Marked Length Spectrum Rigidity of Surfaces*.

Prior to my MSc, I completed a BSc in mathematical physics, also at Queen's University. My BSc thesis *Fuzzy Dark Matter Dynamics and the Quasi-Particle Hypothesis* was supervised by [Lawrence Widrow](https://www.queensu.ca/physics/people-search/lawrence-m-widrow). This was a project in computational astrophysics. It resulted in my first (and as yet only) publication.
